**§ 1 · Executive Summary**

# 01 — The agent stack is fragmenting. Be the glue.

> ChainBridge is the unopinionated middleware layer that composes the six standards every autonomous agent on Ethereum needs — TypeScript-first, MIT-licensed, audited, and economically aligned with payment volume rather than seat licenses.

> **The core insight**
>
> The AI agent stack on Ethereum in 2026 is fragmenting the same way the ERC token stack fragmented in 2018. The teams that build the unifying infrastructure now will own developer mindshare for the next decade. There is a 12 to 18 month window before one stack wins.
>
> ChainBridge does not compete with Coinbase (x402), Anthropic (MCP), Google (A2A), or the Ethereum Foundation (ERC-8004). It glues them together and takes a small fee on the payment volume that flows through.
